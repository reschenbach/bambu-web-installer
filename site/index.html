Bambu Monitor / XIAO ESP32-C3

USB Firmware Installer

Connect your XIAO ESP32-C3 to your Windows PC using a data-capable USB-C cable, then use Chrome or Edge.

1. Install firmware

Use desktop Chrome or Edge with Web Serial enabled.Web Serial requires HTTPS (or localhost).

This erases/replaces the existing firmware and configuration on the selected ESP32. Select the correct serial port.

2. Configure over Wi-Fi

  1. After flashing, disconnect and reconnect the ESP32 USB power.
  2. On your phone, join Wi-Fi Bambu-Monitor-Setup using password BambuSetup2026.
  3. Visit http://192.168.4.1/ and enter Wi-Fi, Bambu P1S and Thingino settings.
  4. Reconnect your phone to your home Wi-Fi, then open the ESP32's assigned IP address.

Local network / WireGuard only. Do not forward port 80 to the public internet. This is experimental firmware, and large MQTT messages may exceed ESP32-C3 memory.
